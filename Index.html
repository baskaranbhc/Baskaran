About Me

I am Dr. A. Baskaran, an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biotechnology at Bon Secours College for Women (Autonomous), Thanjavur. I hold a Ph.D. in Botany from Bharathidasan University, Tiruchirappalli, where my doctoral work examined the phytochemistry, antimicrobial and anticancer activity, and DNA barcoding of Barleria longiflora L.f.

My research connects traditional medicinal plants with modern molecular tools. I have published widely on GC-MS profiling, antimicrobial screening, anticancer studies on HeLa cells, and phylogenetic analysis using matK and rbcL sequences, and I have deposited gene sequences in GenBank. My interests include rDNA technology, molecular and cell biology, microbiology, plant taxonomy, biofertilizers and biological control of pollution.

In the classroom, I aim to make laboratory science clear and practical for students. I have also served as a department NAAC coordinator, helped organise conferences and faculty development programmes, and I enjoy building digital learning resources for my students.
